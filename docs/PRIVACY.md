# Privacy

This page matches the product in 0.2.6 and the public notice at https://vitalog.cc/privacy. It is not a legal opinion and it is not a compliance certificate.

**Last aligned:** 16 Oct 2026  
**Operator:** BSOC (Raghavendra Pratap)  
**Contact:** outreach@bsoc.space

## On the device

- Profile details the person enters
- Lab values, ranges, and flags
- Photos and PDFs of reports, visits, and treatment files, in an encrypted store
- Visits, medicine lists, conditions, and reminders
- The app-lock PIN and the biometric preference, in the platform key store

Vitalog does not run an account server for this health data.

## Not done

- Health data is not sold
- Ads are not based on health data
- Analytics and crash SDKs are not used to upload lab contents
- Flags are not a medical diagnosis

## Permissions

Camera and photos are optional, for attaching a report or reading text on the device. Biometrics are optional, for unlocking the app. The biometric template stays with the operating system.

## Sharing and deletion

The app does not send the health record to Vitalog. The person may export or share a file with another app. That share is their choice.

Settings can wipe Vitalog’s data on this device. Uninstalling the app removes the local data, subject to the operating system. Backup files the person saved elsewhere have to be deleted by them. Vitalog cannot erase those copies.

## Children

Vitalog is for an adult managing family records. A profile for a child needs the authority required by local law.

## Health Connect and Apple Health

If the person connects either store, Vitalog can import weight, glucose, and blood pressure, and can write back a reading they entered. That stays between this device and the health store they already use.
