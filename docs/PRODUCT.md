# Product

Vitalog keeps a family’s lab results, visits, medicines, and reminders on the device. A result is compared with the range printed on that lab report. The app tracks. It does not diagnose a condition or recommend treatment.

Published by BSOC. Privacy contact: outreach@bsoc.space.  
Site: https://vitalog.cc/ · Privacy: https://vitalog.cc/privacy

## Who it is for

An adult keeping records for themselves or for family members. It is for someone who will not put those records on a company server.

## What shipping 0.2.6 does

- Profiles, and switching between people
- Lab panels entered with the range printed on the report
- Trends and flags: high, low, or in range
- Visits, medicines, and reminders
- A report photo or PDF, with a match the person confirms before it is saved
- App lock with a PIN. Biometrics are optional
- An encrypted backup on this device, a wipe, and a restore
- A readable export for the person’s own files
- Optional Health Connect or Apple Health for weight, glucose, and blood pressure on the phone
- Optional pairing with another Vitalog device on the same Wi-Fi
- Reference updates for the test catalogue and medicine names, from vitalog.cc

## What it does not do

- No Vitalog account, and no server that stores the health record
- No ads, and no sale of health data
- No diagnosis, risk score, or treatment advice
- No scheduled cloud backup. A backup file stays where the person saves it
- Desktop builds are unsigned until Apple and Windows signing are in place

Android distribution through Play is waiting on an organisation account. Desktop delivery is the current public download.
