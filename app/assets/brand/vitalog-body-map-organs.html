For developers

One SVG in the same 180 × 300 coordinate space as the original body-map mockup. Each system is a group with the id sys-<system_code> (codes from system_info in catalog.db). Set its class to ok, high, low or nothing (not tested), or off when the user hides it in Customize body panel. Shapes marked data-sex show only for that sex.

  • Fill parts carry class s; line parts (veins, outline, links) carry class ln and take the colour as a stroke.
  • High and low get a soft halo (.halo) so a flagged organ is found at a glance, as the heart already does.
  • Tiny organs (pituitary, parathyroid, adrenal, lymph nodes) are too small to tap reliably. Make the tap target at least 44 px around them, or open the list row instead.
  • The shapes are simple symbols, not anatomy. They follow the existing style: flat fills, no outlines.
